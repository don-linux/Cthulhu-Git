## Changed

- **The branch and commit sidebars open at their default width once after updating.** egui 0.37 identifies a panel by where it sits in the window as well as by its name, so the widths 0.0.8 saved no longer match. Drag them again and they are remembered as before. By @don-linux. (#20)
- **Interface icons and the logo are drawn from their SVG files** at the size and display scale they are shown at, so they stay sharp on high-DPI screens. By @don-linux. (#20)
- **Text in a script no bundled font covers, such as Chinese, Japanese or Korean, is drawn with the system's fonts** when it has them, instead of boxes. Emoji still come from the bundled monochrome fonts, so they look the same everywhere. By @don-linux. (#20)
